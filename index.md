## About Me

I am Chief Legal Officer at [Scrydon](https://scrydon.com/), an enterprise AI platform combining human-AI collaboration, agentic orchestration, advanced analytics, and sovereign infrastructure to automate processes and deliver knowledge at scale. I am teaching AI Governance at the UC Law SF and work with [SaferAI](https://www.safer-ai.org/) to advance AI safety. I am currently also working on another start up in the agentic AI security space. My interest centers on the intersection of technology law, AI advancements, and AI safety. I am a board member of the IAPP AI Governance Centre and a member of the OECD’s AI expert network. I have contributed to the GPAI Code of Practice and currently participate in the Transparency Code of Practice — both convened under the EU AI Act — as well as the EU AI Office’s Frontier AI Expert Group. Previously, I led Microsoft’s Responsible Tech and Competition team in Europe and headed the legal department of the European Consumer Organization BEUC. I am a qualified German lawyer and hold degrees from the University of Hamburg and the University of Strathclyde in Glasgow (LL.M. Tech Law). Fomalhaut is my management company, operating across advisory services, research & speaking engagements, teaching, and art.

I am advising on AI compliance, Tech policy, and policy strategy. Reach out if you'd like to discuss confined projects.
