## About Me

I am Chief Legal Officer at [Scrydon](https://scrydon.com/), an enterprise AI platform combining human-AI collaboration, agentic orchestration, advanced analytics, and sovereign infrastructure to automate processes and deliver knowledge at scale. I am teaching AI Governance at [UC Law SF](https://www.uclawsf.edu/people/cornelia-kutterer-2/) and work with [SaferAI](https://www.safer-ai.org/) to advance AI safety. I am also supporting an early-stage startup in the agentic AI security space, [Boundary Labs AI](https://boundarylabs.ai/). My interest centers on the intersection of technology law, AI advancements, and AI safety. I am a board member of the IAPP AI Governance Centre and a member of the OECD’s AI expert network. I have contributed to the GPAI Code of Practice and participated in the Transparency Code of Practice — both convened under the EU AI Act — as well as the EU AI Office’s Frontier AI Expert Group. Previously, I led Microsoft’s Responsible Tech and Competition team in Europe and headed the legal department of the European Consumer Organization BEUC. I am a qualified German lawyer and hold degrees from the University of Hamburg and the University of Strathclyde in Glasgow (LL.M. Tech Law). Fomalhaut is my management company, through which I provide services across advisory, research, speaking engagements, teaching, and art.

I am advising on AI compliance, Tech policy, and policy strategy. Reach out if you'd like to discuss confined projects.
